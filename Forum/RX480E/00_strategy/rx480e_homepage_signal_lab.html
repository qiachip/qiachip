04 / FIRST TEST

Prove the signal before the full project.

A good first test answers one question: does the expected RX480E output pin change when you press the remote?

Run Your First Test
01

Power and pair

Use the correct voltage for your model and pair a compatible transmitter.

02

Press one button

Test one remote input at a time so the resulting output pin is clear.

03

Check the output

Use an LED or multimeter to confirm whether the expected OUT or D0-D3 pin changes.

Do not use a motor, lock, pump, high-current relay coil, or mains device as the first test load.
